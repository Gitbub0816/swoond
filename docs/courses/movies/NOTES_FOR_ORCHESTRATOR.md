1. **Horror Films boundary decision (applies spec section 6): `horror-films` stays an independent Wave 3 course, NOT a branch of `movies`.** Test result: learning Movies gives film language and genre-as-promise, but not horror-fandom competence (its own canon, subgenre taxonomy, franchise sagas, practical-effects and gore culture, festivals, "elevated horror" fight). Consequences: Movies teaches horror as one genre at survey depth (`gn-06`, one lesson plus say-this items, ending in a hand-off card); the `genre` personalization dimension set to horror surfaces the hand-off and horror-skewed examples in film-language lessons. `horror-films` may assume Movies' `reading-the-frame` and `motion-cut-and-sound` concepts (cross-link, no hard prerequisite). **Action:** update the `horror-films` row in `CATALOG.md` boundary notes to "independent; cross-links to `movies`", and confirm with the product owner (CDS open question 1).
