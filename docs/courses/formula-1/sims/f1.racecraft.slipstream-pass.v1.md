## 3. Learning objective(s) & concepts taught
- **Learner-facing objective:** "You can use a car's slipstream to close in, choose when to pull out, and know when the smart move is to wait."

| conceptId | Term | After this sim the learner can... | Assessed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `slipstream` | Slipstream (tow) | Explain that following closely on a straight cuts drag and lifts top speed, and time the pull-out | Yes (primary) |
| `dirty-air` | Dirty air | Explain that following closely through a corner loses downforce, so exit speed suffers | Yes |
| `drag` | Drag | Connect the tow to reduced drag | Yes (light) |
| `braking-zone` | Braking zone | Recognise that the pass is decided by who is alongside at the braking point | Yes |
| `dive-bomb` | Dive-bomb | Identify a lunge without overlap and why it is risky | Yes |
| `overtake-mode` | Overtake Mode | See that 2026 Overtake Mode adds speed when within one second, and that it does not replace the tow | Yes (d4+) |
| `gap-and-interval` | Gap and interval | Read a gap in car lengths or seconds | Shown |
| `defending-position` | Defending | See the leader cover a line and why an early move gets covered | Shown |

- **Out of scope:** steering, throttle or brake control, tyre wear, DRS (retired), team orders, penalties (`stewards` is native), wet racing and multi-car battles. The sim is a chase model, not a driving game.

## 4. Why Unity (tier justification)
- **Rubric answer:** Movement over time and physics both materially help. The tow is a positional effect between two moving cars (alignment and distance change drag frame by frame), and "when to pull out" is a timing decision inside a moving scene. The camera perspective (chase-high, then top-down at the braking marker) shows overlap, which is the concept.
- **Closest native alternatives:** `binary-call` on a static diagram ("pull out now?") can show a rule but cannot show speed accumulating or the wake; `timing-tap` is a 1D bar without track, gap or wake context. A native `decision-scenario` is used in `rc-04` (defending rules) where dynamics do not matter.
- **Verdict:** Justified; anchor sim for the racecraft unit.

## 5. Player fantasy & core loop
- **Fantasy:** You are the driver on the tail of the car ahead, waiting for the right moment.
- **Core loop (3 rounds, about 3 minutes):**
  1. Prompt: a corner exit with a car ahead: "Stay close, or give it room?" (d2+).
  2. Decisive interaction (one per phase): choose the follow distance through the corner; then on the straight, tap **Pull left / Pull right** at the right moment, or **Stay behind**.
  3. Execute: the cars run to the braking marker; a side-by-side top-down freeze shows the overlap.
  4. Freeze/explain: wake cone, speed difference, overlap ruler in car lengths.
  5. Line to say: "He got a great tow down the straight."
- **One mechanic:** choose when (and whether) to leave the tow.

## 7. Controls (touch)
| Input | Gesture / target | Hit size | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corner decision | Tap **Stay close** or **Give it room** | 56 pt pill | wake and dirty zones fade in; haptic light tap |
| Pull out left/right | Tap the left/right bottom pill **at the moment** | 56 pt | the car slides across over 0.7 s; wake cone loses strength; haptic tick at the start |
| Stay behind | Tap the centre pill, or do nothing until the braking marker | 56 pt | car brakes behind the leader |
| Replay | Tap "Replay" in Explain | 44 pt | 0.5x broadcast-side |

- **Alternative tap-only scheme (`controlScheme: "tap-anywhere"`):** a single large target; tap once = "Pull out on the inside line", no side choice; "Stay behind" is a second target. Also removes the timing pressure by offering a **step-through mode** (`timingAssist: true`): the sim pauses at each 0.5 s and asks "Pull out now?" Yes/No.
- **Orientation:** portrait; buttons in the bottom safe area.
- **Native UI not drawn by Unity:** paywall, hearts, exit confirmation.

## 11. Scenario data set
- **Format:** JSON (`slipstream-starter.json`), 14 scenarios (N >= rounds x 3 = 9). Deterministic per seed (order without replacement; scenario parameters fixed).
- **Scenario schema:** `{ id, name, tags[], straightM, entrySpeedMps, cornerDirtyPenaltyMps, overtakeModeEligible, defender: {covers, coverWindowS}, cornerSide, correct: {follow, action, sideBest}, teachingPoint, conceptId }`.
- **Physics model (normative):**
  - Longitudinal acceleration for each car: `a = A - K*(1-tau)*v^2 - taper(v)`; `A = 11 m/s^2`, `K = A / 88^2`; the leader has `tau = 0`; `taper(v) = 3.2 * clamp((v - vStart)/(97 - vStart), 0, 1)` with `vStart = 80.5 m/s`; for a car with Overtake Mode active, `vStart = 93.6 m/s`. (Electric assist tapers to zero by roughly 350 km/h; Overtake Mode moves the taper start to about 337 km/h; simplified.)
  - Tow factor `tau = 0.10 * fg * fl`, where `fg = clamp(1 - (gapM - 5)/35, 0, 1)` (gapM is bumper gap; `fg = 1` when the gap is 0 or closer) and `fl = max(1 - lat/2.2, 0)` (lat is lateral offset in metres).
  - Lateral move: on tap, `lat` rises to 2.6 m over 0.7 s (linear).
  - Corner phase: options **Stay close** (start gap 8 m, exit-speed penalty `1.0 * P`) and **Give it room** (start gap 14 m, penalty `0.30 * P`), where `P = cornerDirtyPenaltyMps`. Player starts at `entrySpeedMps - penalty`; the leader at `entrySpeedMps`.
  - Overtake Mode is active for the player if `overtakeModeEligible` and the start gap in seconds (`gapM / entrySpeed`) <= 1.0 s at the straight's detection line (always true for both options at these gaps).
  - Progress `p = (playerFront - leadFront) / 5.5 m` at the braking marker (positive = ahead).
  - Pass outcome at the marker for the tapped side: **inside** (the corner side): clean if `p >= 0.25` and `tPbS >= claimTimeS`; late lunge if `-0.5 <= p < 0.25` or `tPbS < claimTimeS`; no pass if `p < -0.5`. **Outside** (opposite the corner): clean if `p >= 0.8`; else squeezed wide (fail). **Covered:** if the defender covers and `tPbS > coverWindowS`, the leader moves to the side and the pass fails (`p` reset to the pre-move value).
  - The cars run `dt = 0.01 s` fixed-step; deterministic.
- **Indicative results at `tPbS` = 1.0 s (prototype, +/-0.1; Astra regenerates golden values):** `p` for Stay close / Give it room.

## 12. Freeze / explain moments
Trigger: braking marker reached. Camera: top-down. Highlight: gold pulse on the overlap ruler; rose outline on the learner's car; callouts one at a time. Limits: Title <= 6 words; body <= 45 words.

## 13. Scoring & mastery signals
- **Round score:** Clean pass with the best side and best follow choice = 100; clean pass (inside, less-preferred follow choice) = 85; clean outside pass = 70; correct hold = 90; missed chance (held when a clean pass was available) = 40; late lunge = 20; no pass or covered after pulling = 30; corner choice bonus: +0 (folded into outcomes).
- **Round success:** score >= 70. **Session score:** mean, rounded. **Accuracy:** successful rounds / rounds. **Outcome ids:** `round-1`..`round-N` (`value` = scenarioId).

| Mistake | conceptId | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Tapped with `tPbS` > coverWindow when the defender covers | `defending-position` | "Pulled out early and got covered." |
| Tapped with `tPbS` > 3.0 s (any straight) | `slipstream` | "Left the tow too early." |
| Tapped with `tPbS` < claimTime | `dive-bomb` | "Went for a gap that was not there." |
| Chose Stay close where Give it room was better | `dirty-air` | "Followed too close in dirty air." |
| Chose Stay behind when a clean pass was available | `slipstream` | "Missed a pass the tow offered." |
| In an OM scenario, held or pulled early without using the boost window | `overtake-mode` | "Did not use Overtake Mode." |

## 14. XP & hearts

- `replayAvailable`: true after the first Explain.

## 16. Accessibility
- **Reduced motion:** cuts between camera presets; no shake; freeze is a hard cut; lateral slide is a 0.2 s cross-fade of car positions.
- **Haptics off:** visual equivalents (pill flash, gauge pulse).
- **Colour-blind:** wake cone uses hatch pattern intensity plus a numeric tow readout (`+x km/h`); car identities by number ring and shape; window bracket has an icon and label.
- **Text scale:** overlay text scales to 200%.
- **Tap-only / low dexterity:** `tap-anywhere` scheme plus `timingAssist`.
- **VoiceOver:** Unity content is limited. Native fallback lesson `rc-02-alt-tow-diagram` (binary-call: "Pull out now?" on static frames with alt text, plus a `decision-scenario` on when to hold) with equal mastery credit for `slipstream`. A separate designed exercise, not a port.
