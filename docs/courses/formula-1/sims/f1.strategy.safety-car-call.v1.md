## 3. Learning objective(s) & concepts taught
- **Learner-facing objective:** "You can explain why a stop under the safety car is cheap, and make the call to pit or stay out with tyre age and track position in mind."

| conceptId | Term | After this sim the learner can... | Assessed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `safety-car-stop` | Safety car stop | Explain that the field is slowed and bunched, so a stop costs roughly half as much | Yes (primary) |
| `safety-car` | Safety car | See that the pack compresses behind the safety car | Yes |
| `virtual-safety-car` | Virtual Safety Car | Know that gaps stay frozen and a stop is cheaper, but less than under a full safety car | Yes (d3+) |
| `pit-loss` | Pit loss | See loss shrink from about 21 s to about 10 s (SC) or 14 s (VSC) | Yes |
| `track-position` | Track position | See that leaders with fresh tyres may stay out to keep the lead | Yes |
| `double-stack` | Double stack | Understand the cost of pitting both cars on the same lap | Yes (d4+) |
| `tyre-degradation` | Tyre degradation | Use tyre age and the laps left to decide | Yes |
| `tyre-cliff` | Tyre cliff | Recognise that old tyres near the cliff must stop | Yes |

- **Out of scope:** why the safety car was deployed, restart procedures (`standing-restart` is native), lapped-car rules, pit-lane closures under red flags, penalties, weather. The sim treats the SC as an event that has already happened.

## 4. Why Unity (tier justification)
- **Rubric answer:** A decision depends on reading a dynamic scene under time pressure: the field bunching behind the safety car is spatial (cars compress into a train), and the result of "pit or stay out" (where you rejoin among the others) only appears when the cars move. Camera perspective (top-down train, then rejoin) matters.
- **Closest native alternatives:** a `decision-scenario` fact sheet ("gap 1.5 s, tyre age 20, SC out") teaches the rule that stops are cheap and is used natively elsewhere; `binary-call` cannot show the bunching or the rejoin. The sim is used where seeing the field compress and the rejoin position is the point.
- **Verdict:** Justified. If a lesson needs only the rule, use the native scenario.

## 5. Player fantasy & core loop
- **Fantasy:** The safety car is out and the radio is waiting for your call.
- **Core loop (3 rounds, about 3 minutes):**
  1. Prompt: freeze at SC deployment with a fact sheet: "Lap 22. SC out. Your tyres: Medium, 20 laps old."
  2. Decisive interaction: tap **Stay out** or **Pit** (and a compound); d4+: also choose the second car's plan.
  3. Execute: the field bunches behind the safety car; your car pits or stays; the restart happens; the race runs to the flag.
  4. Freeze/explain: the running order before and after; a "who gained what" strip.
  5. Line to say: "He pitted under the safety car and it was basically free."
- **One mechanic:** the pit-or-stay-out call.

## 11. Scenario data set
- **Format:** JSON `sc-call-starter.json`, 14 scenarios (>= rounds x 3). Deterministic per seed.
- **Scenario schema:** `{ id, name, tags[], mode:"SC"|"VSC", neutralLap, neutralLaps, totalLaps, leader:"P"|"R", gapS, cars:{P:{comp,age},R:{comp,age,plan:[{lap,comp}]},P2?:{comp,age,gapS}}, teachingPoint, conceptId }`.
- **Race model:** identical lap-time and tyre model to `f1.strategy.pit-window.v1` section 11 (compounds S/M/H, cliff cap 3.0 s, pit loss 21.0 s, in-lap 0.8 s, out-lap 1.3 s, hard-to-pass rule `passMarginS` 1.2 outside neutralisations). Neutralisation additions (normative):
  - **SC:** for `neutralLaps` laps every car runs `130.0 s` laps; tyres do not age; cars that do not pit are compressed behind the leader at `0.8 s` spacing; a car that pits pays `21.0 * 0.45` s (about 9.45 s); the out-lap adds 0.5 s.
  - **VSC:** for `neutralLaps` laps every car runs `126 s` laps; gaps are preserved; a pit costs `21.0 * 0.65` s (about 13.65 s).
  - **Double stack:** if the second car pits on the same lap as the first, it pays an extra `3.0 s` (waiting for the crew).
  - The player's alternatives after the neutralisation: stay out and choose the best later green-flag stop (or no stop) using the golden optimiser; the round's `M(option)` is the margin (seconds) over the reference rival at the flag.
- **Scoring reference:** golden tool computes `M` for every option; `M*` is the best. **Score:** 100 if `M*-M <= 0.5`; 70 if within the level's tolerance (section 9); 40 if within 8 s; else 10.
- **Indicative margins (prototype; treat as ordering, cliff penalties exaggerate large gaps):** margin of P over R at the flag in seconds for Stay out / Pit Hard / Pit Medium.

## 12. Freeze / explain moments
Trigger: chequered flag after the restart. Camera: `top-down` with the order strip; replay the pit exit at 0.5x. Limits: Title <= 6 words; body <= 45 words.

| Case | Callouts | Title | Body | Say this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pitted, worked | Pit-loss card (about 10 s vs 21 s); order strip up | "A stop that costs half." | "With the field slowed behind the safety car, the pit stop cost about half its usual time. You got fresh tyres and lost little track position. That is why everyone piles in." | "He pitted under the safety car and it was basically free." |
| Stayed, should have pitted | Tyre age bar; cliff marker | "You paid for it later." | "Your tyres were old and you passed up the cheap stop. You had to pay full price later or run on tyres past the cliff. When stops are cheap, old tyres should take them." | "He didn't pit and it cost him." |
| Stayed, was right | Order strip stable; lead highlighted | "Keeping the lead was right." | "Your tyres were fresh and you led. A stop would have dropped you behind cars that stayed out. Sometimes the best call is to do nothing." | "He kept track position and stayed out." |
| Pitted, should have stayed | Order strip drops | "Gave the lead away." | "Fresh tyres and a lead: you did not need the stop, and pitting cost you positions. Look at tyre age before you box." | "He pitted for nothing." |
| VSC vs SC | Two pit-loss cards | "Virtual is not the same." | "A Virtual Safety Car freezes the gaps but does not bunch the field. A stop costs less than green, yet more than under a full safety car. The bunching is what makes the safety car special." | "Under the VSC the gaps stay the same." |
| Double stack | Second car waiting | "Two cars, one crew." | "Both cars pitted on the same lap, so the second waited in the box and lost about three seconds. Splitting the stops across the safety-car laps avoids it, if the safety car lasts." | "They double-stacked and the second car lost time." |
| Timeout (d3+) | Timer ring empty | "The clock beat you." | "No call was made, so the car stayed out by default. Real strategists have seconds. Trust the tyre age and the gap." | "The call came too late." |
| Summary (good) | Chips | "You made the calls." | "You read tyre age, the gap and the cost of a stop. Next time a fan says 'they got a free stop', you will know why it was free." | "It was a free stop under the safety car." |
| Summary (needs work) | Best round replay | "Nearly. One more call?" | "Ask three things: how old are my tyres, who is behind me, and how many laps are left? The answer usually shows itself." | "What were his tyres like?" |

## 13. Scoring & mastery signals
- **Round score:** as section 11. **Session score:** mean. **Accuracy:** successful rounds (score >= 70) / rounds. **Outcome ids:** `round-1`..`round-N` (`value` = scenarioId and choice).

| Mistake | conceptId | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Stayed out with tyres beyond the cliff lap by the flag | `tyre-cliff` | "Stayed out on tyres that could not last." |
| Pitted from the lead with fresh tyres when staying was best | `track-position` | "Gave away the lead for nothing." |
| Pitted when the golden best was to stay (VSC/SC) with margin loss >= 3 s | `safety-car-stop` | "Paid a stop that was not needed." |
| Stayed out when the golden best was to pit with margin loss >= 3 s | `safety-car-stop` | "Passed up a cheap stop." |
| Double-stacked (same lap) when a split was available | `double-stack` | "Lost time waiting in the box." |
| Timer expired (d3+) | none (`timeout` flag) | "No call made." |

| Mastery event | conceptId | delta | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score >= 70 in a SC scenario | `safety-car-stop` | +0.20 | "Made a good safety-car call." |
| Score >= 70 in a VSC scenario | `virtual-safety-car` | +0.15 | "Read the VSC correctly." |
| Score >= 70 when the best call was stay out | `track-position` | +0.15 | "Kept track position." |
| Split stop when available (d4+) | `double-stack` | +0.10 | "Avoided the double-stack loss." |
| Round success | `pit-loss` | +0.10 | "Used the reduced pit loss." |
| Old tyres pitted before cliff | `tyre-cliff` | +0.10 | "Renewed tyres in time." |
| Round failed | `safety-car-stop` | -0.10 | "Misjudged the safety-car stop." |
| Hints used | round concepts | halve positives | native halves mastery gain |

- **Caps:** +/-0.30 per concept per session. Standard mapping to `outcomes[]`, `mistakes[]`, `masterySignals[]`.

## 16. Accessibility
- **Reduced motion:** freeze is a hard cut; the bunching is shown as a cross-fade from spread to train; no shake.
- **Haptics off:** visual equivalents (pill pulse).
- **Colour-blind:** compounds use letters plus colour; cars identified by number and shape; the safety car has a stripe pattern and label.
- **Text scale:** to 200%; the fact sheet reflows.
- **Tap-only:** the sim is tap-only; `decisionTimerSeconds: null` and `paceAssist` remove time pressure.
- **VoiceOver:** Unity content is limited. Native fallback lesson `sd-04-alt-safety-car` (a `decision-scenario` with the fact sheet and graded options) with equal mastery credit for `safety-car-stop`. A separate designed exercise, not a port.

## 21. Acceptance criteria (testable)
- **AC-1:** Seed 3, difficulty 1, 3 rounds emits 3 `outcomes` and one schema-valid `result`.
- **AC-2:** Determinism: same seed and choices produce identical margins (1e-6) and signals.
- **AC-3:** For `sc-both-need-stop` the golden ordering is Pit Medium > Pit Hard > Stay out.
- **AC-4:** For `sc-fresh-leader-stays` the golden best is Stay out.
- **AC-5:** SC pit loss is `21.0 * 0.45` s; VSC is `21.0 * 0.65` s; the double-stack adds 3.0 s to the second car.
- **AC-6:** Under an SC, non-pitting cars end each SC lap at 0.8 s spacing; under a VSC gaps are preserved.
- **AC-7:** Timeout (d3+) applies "Stay out" and flags `timeout`.
- **AC-8:** Copy: every title <= 6 words; every body <= 45 words.
- **AC-9:** All mastery `conceptId`s exist in the curriculum concept list.
- **AC-10:** Reduced motion: no eased time-scale ramp, no shake.
- **AC-11:** Targets >= 44 pt; the sim completes with taps only and with the timer disabled.
- **AC-12:** `pause`, `resume`, `abort` per the bridge README.
- **AC-13:** Budgets on iPhone 13-class: p5 fps >= 50, memory < 110 MB, `launch` -> `ready` < 2 s.
- **AC-14:** Invalid config emits `error CONFIG_INVALID`.
