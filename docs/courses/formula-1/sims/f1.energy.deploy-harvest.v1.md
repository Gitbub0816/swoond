## 3. Learning objective(s) & concepts taught
- **Learner-facing objective:** "You can explain why a 2026 car cannot use electrical power everywhere, and where on a lap it is worth spending."

| conceptId | Term | After this sim the learner can... | Assessed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `energy-store` | Energy store (battery) | Manage a finite battery over a lap and end near a target | Yes (primary) |
| `recharge` | Recharge (harvesting) | Explain that braking refills the battery, and there are limits per lap | Yes |
| `super-clipping` | Clipping | Recognise the car running out of assist on a straight and why it slows | Yes |
| `boost-button` | Boost | Understand the driver's manual deployment as spending a limited budget | Yes |
| `mgu-k` | MGU-K | See deployment (up to 350 kW) and recovery in the same motor-generator | Shown |
| `overtake-mode` | Overtake Mode | See that within one second at the detection line, extra deployment is available | Yes (d5) |
| `lift-and-coast` | Lift and coast | See it offered as the alternative when the battery is low | Shown |
| `ice` | Combustion engine | Understand the engine alone holds less speed than engine plus battery | Shown |

- **Out of scope:** real MJ figures and regulation limits (abstract units), fuel-flow limits, gearing, cooling, engine modes, qualifying rules beyond a simple regen scale, and the driver's other controls. The car deploys automatically in the real world; the sim simplifies this to one manual deploy control to teach the trade-off.

## 4. Why Unity (tier justification)
- **Rubric answer:** Physics and timing in a scene both materially help. The concept is that a finite battery has to be placed along a lap. Speed changes along the track, a taper reduces value at high speed, and running dry shows as the car losing assist at the end of a straight. That is felt, not read: a ghost car and speed trace make the difference visible. Camera perspective is supportive (chase view, top-down explain).
- **Closest native alternatives:** `estimate-slider` and `sequence-order` can state rules and quantities; `decision-scenario` can present a scenario with a fact sheet. None can put the learner's choices onto a moving lap where the answer emerges from speed and battery over distance. Native lessons in the same unit cover the rules.
- **Verdict:** Justified; the anchor sim for the 2026 energy story.

## 5. Player fantasy & core loop
- **Fantasy:** You are the driver with a limited battery and a lap to win.
- **Core loop (3 laps, about 3 minutes):**
  1. Prompt: "Spend it where it counts. Do not run dry."
  2. Decisive interaction: **hold** the Deploy button to use battery power; release to save it.
  3. Execute: the car drives the lap with automatic braking and automatic recharge; a grey ghost car ran with no deployment.
  4. Freeze/explain: at lap end, the speed trace, battery trace and the time gained per unit of energy in each spot.
  5. Line to say: "He clipped at the end of the straight."
- **One mechanic:** hold to deploy.

## 11. Scenario data set
- **Format:** JSON `energy-starter.json`, 9 scenarios (>= rounds x 3). Deterministic per seed.
- **Scenario schema:** `{ id, name, tags[], segments:[{kind:"straight",lengthM}|{kind:"corner",radiusM,arcM}], startSoc, floorSoc, regenScale, tNeverS, tBestS, teachingPoint, conceptId, lead? }`.
- **Physics model (normative):**
  - State `x, v, soc`. Non-braking acceleration: `a = A_ice - Kd*v^2 [+ AE*taper(v)]` with `A_ice = 6.5 m/s^2`, `Kd = 6.5 / 75^2` (the engine alone holds about 270 km/h), `AE = 5.5 m/s^2`, `taper(v) = 1` for `v <= 80.5 m/s`, linear to 0 at 97 m/s (electric assist tapers at high speed). Speed cap 97 m/s.
  - Deploy (button held, on a straight, not braking, `soc > 2`): adds `AE*taper(v)` and drains `10 %/s`. If `soc <= 2` while held: **clip**: `a = A_ice - Kd*v^2 - 2.0` and `soc` rises `4 %/s` (the engine harvests instead of pushing).
  - Braking: automatic at 45 m/s^2 to the corner speed `vc = sqrt(40*R)`; regen `+8.5 * regenScale %/s` while braking; `soc` capped at 100.
  - Score penalty: `0.15 s` per percentage point of final `soc` below `floorSoc`.
  - Overtake Mode (`attack` scenarios): armed if the time gap to `car_lead` <= 1.0 s at the start of the marked straight; then deploy on that straight uses `AE = 7.0` and taper start 93.6 m/s (Overtake Mode moves the taper start to about 337 km/h).
  - Fixed step 0.01 s; deterministic.
- **Score:** `score = 100 * clamp(1 - (tLearner + penalty - tBest)/D, 0, 1)`; `D = max(Dfloor, 0.5 * (tNever - tBest))`.
- **Indicative golden values (prototype, +/-0.2 s; Astra regenerates via a coordinate-ascent optimiser):**

| scenarioId | Setup | Teaches | tNever / tBest (s) | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `three-straights-teach` | S600, R30, S800, R45, S500, R30 | Spend after slow corners, not at the end | 41.88 / 39.68 | teach |
| `clipping-trap` | S1200, R35, S400, R30 | Holding on empty slows you (clip) | 31.58 / 29.44 | teach |
| `two-slow-exits` | S600 R25 S700 R80 S700 R35 | Slow-corner exits are worth more | 42.28 / 40.30 | street |

The value is in where and when, not how much.

## 13. Scoring & mastery signals
- **Round score:** as section 11. **Session score:** mean. **Accuracy:** successful rounds / rounds. **Outcome ids:** `round-1`..`round-N` (`value` = scenarioId).

| Mistake | conceptId | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Clip time >= 2 s | `super-clipping` | "Held deploy with an empty battery." |
| >= 40% of deploy seconds in the last 30% of straights | `boost-button` | "Spent power where it buys least." |
| Final SoC below floor | `energy-store` | "Ended below the target." |
| Final SoC > floor + 25 | `energy-store` | "Left energy unused." |
| Attack scenario: OM not armed (gap > 1.0 s at the line) | `overtake-mode` | "Did not get within a second." |

| Mastery event | conceptId | delta | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero clip time in the round | `super-clipping` | +0.15 | "Never ran dry on a straight." |
| Final SoC in [floor, floor + 10] | `energy-store` | +0.15 | "Ended the lap near the target." |
| >= 70% of deployment within 2 s of slow-corner exits | `boost-button` | +0.10 | "Spent power out of corners." |
| Round success | `recharge` | +0.10 | "Used braking to refill the battery." |
| Overtake Mode pass (d5) | `overtake-mode` | +0.15 | "Got within a second and used the boost." |
| Clip >= 2 s | `super-clipping` | -0.10 | "Ran the battery dry." |
| Ended below floor | `energy-store` | -0.10 | "Spent too much." |
| Hints used | round concepts | halve positives | native halves mastery gain |

- **Caps:** +/-0.30 per concept per session. Standard mapping to `outcomes[]`, `mistakes[]`, `masterySignals[]`.

## 14. XP & hearts
- `xpEarned`: +10 per successful round; +40 for finishing; +5 flawless bonus at d >= 3. Native clamps.
- `heartsLost`: 1 if two or more rounds fail; max 1.

## 16. Accessibility
- **Reduced motion:** freeze is a hard cut; no shake; the battery bar changes without easing (numeral updates).
- **Haptics off:** visual equivalents (button pulse, battery bar flash).
- **Colour-blind:** the value ribbon uses thickness plus a pattern (dots density) in addition to colour; clip warning is a light plus label "CLIP"; rose vs gold traces differ by dash.
- **Text scale:** to 200%; the battery numeral uses Numeral serif and scales.
- **Tap-only:** `toggle` scheme and `autoStopAtFloor`.
- **VoiceOver:** Unity content is limited. Native fallback lesson `r26-06-alt-energy-plan` (a `sequence-order` "when do you deploy?" and `estimate-slider` "how long does it last?") with equal mastery credit for `energy-store`. Separate designed exercise, not a port.

## 21. Acceptance criteria (testable)
- **AC-1:** Seed 5, difficulty 2, 3 rounds emits 3 `outcomes` and one schema-valid `result`.
- **AC-2:** Determinism: same seed and scripted inputs give identical times (1e-6 s) and signals.
- **AC-3:** For `lap-standard`, "never deploy" gives `tNever` within 0.2 s of golden; "deploy 1.5 s after each corner exit" gives within 0.3 s of `tBest`.
- **AC-4:** Holding deploy for the entire lap yields clip time > 10 s and a floor penalty; the round scores below 60 at d3.
- **AC-5:** Clip: with `soc <= 2` and the button held, acceleration equals `A_ice - Kd*v^2 - 2.0` and SoC rises 4 %/s.
- **AC-6:** Braking regen: `+8.5 * regenScale %/s`; `regenScale` 0.875 reduces regen by 12.5%.
- **AC-7:** Copy: every title <= 6 words and body <= 45 words.
- **AC-8:** All mastery `conceptId`s exist in the curriculum concept list.
- **AC-9:** Reduced motion: no eased time-scale ramp, no shake.
- **AC-10:** Targets >= 44 pt; `toggle` and `autoStopAtFloor` complete a lap with taps only.
- **AC-11:** `pause`, `resume`, `abort` per the bridge README.
- **AC-12:** Budgets on iPhone 13-class: p5 fps >= 50, memory < 110 MB, `launch` -> `ready` < 2 s.
- **AC-13:** Invalid config emits `error CONFIG_INVALID`.
- **AC-14:** Overtake Mode arms only when the gap at the marked line is <= 1.0 s.
