## 3. Learning objective(s) & concepts taught
- **Learner-facing objective:** "You can explain how 2026 active aero works: open the wings for speed on straights, close them for grip in the braking zone and corners, and know why the timing matters."

| conceptId | Term | After this sim the learner can... | Assessed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `active-aero` | Active aero | Explain that the wings move during the lap to trade downforce for drag | Yes (primary) |
| `straight-mode` | Straight Mode (X-mode) | Open the wings in a straight-mode zone and see the speed gain | Yes |
| `corner-mode` | Corner Mode (Z-mode) | Close the wings before braking and know why | Yes |
| `drag` | Drag | Link wings open to less drag and higher top speed | Yes |
| `downforce` | Downforce | Link wings open to less grip in the braking zone and corners | Yes |
| `drs` | DRS (retired) | Contrast: DRS was for the chaser only and had a single flap; this is for everyone and both wings | Shown |
| `braking-zone` | Braking zone | Know that wings must be closed by the braking point | Shown |

- **Out of scope:** the FIA's exact zone rules (modelled abstractly), wet-weather restrictions beyond a "disabled zone" case, Overtake Mode and battery energy (covered by `f1.energy.deploy-harvest.v1`), set-up and ride height, and the gap to a car ahead.

## 4. Why Unity (tier justification)
- **Rubric answer:** Timing in a scene and physics both materially help. The wing mode must be timed against the track geometry (zone start, braking point) and the consequence (top speed gained, grip lost) is felt in the car's speed and in a ghost car that ran with the wings always closed. Camera perspective (chase view with a wing schematic) shows the mode change happening.
- **Closest native alternatives:** `timing-tap` is a 1D bar with no track context or speed consequence; `binary-call` ("open or closed here?") states the rule but not the trade-off; a `hotspot-tap` can mark zones on a static circuit map (used natively in `ce-03` as a warm-up). The sim teaches the timing which those cannot.
- **Verdict:** Justified.

## 5. Player fantasy & core loop
- **Fantasy:** You are the driver who decides when the wings open and close.
- **Core loop (3 laps of short circuits, about 3 minutes):**
  1. Prompt: "Open the wings on the straights. Close them before you brake."
  2. Decisive interaction: **hold** the big button to open the wings inside a highlighted zone; **release** to close.
  3. Execute: the car runs the lap; a grey ghost car runs the same lap with the wings always closed.
  4. Freeze/explain at the end of the lap (or at a violation): compare speed traces and the gap to the ghost.
  5. Line to say: "He opened up on the back straight and just flew."
- **One mechanic:** hold to open, release to close.

## 7. Controls (touch)
| Input | Gesture / target | Hit size | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open the wings | **Press and hold** the big button (bottom centre) while in a zone | 88 x 64 pt | wing schematic animates open; drag gauge falls; haptic soft tick at full open |
| Close the wings | Release | n/a | schematic closes over 0.4 s; downforce gauge rises |
| Locked zone press | Press while locked | n/a | button shakes (cross-fade under reduced motion), label "Locked" |
| Replay | Tap in Explain | 44 pt | 0.5x broadcast-side |

- **Tap-toggle alternative (`controlScheme: "toggle"`):** tap once to open and tap again to close (no holding).
- **Assist (`autoCloseAssist: true`):** wings auto-close at the brake board minus 0.4 s; the learner only decides when to open (for motor accessibility).
- **Orientation:** portrait; button inside the bottom safe area.
- **Native UI not drawn by Unity:** paywall, hearts sheet, exit confirmation.

## 11. Scenario data set
- **Format:** JSON `active-modes-starter.json`, 10 scenarios (>= rounds x 3). Deterministic per seed (order without replacement; no random physics).
- **Scenario schema:** `{ id, name, tags[], segments:[{kind:"straight",lengthM,zone:bool,locked?:bool}|{kind:"corner",radiusM,arcM}], tNeverS, tBestS, teachingPoint, conceptId }`.
- **Physics model (normative):**
  - State: position `x`, speed `v`, wing `w` in [0,1] (0 = Corner Mode, 1 = Straight Mode). `w` moves toward the target at `1/actuationS` per second. The target is 1 only while the button is held, the car is on a zone straight (not locked) and not braking.
  - Acceleration when not braking: `a = A - Kd(w)*v^2`; `A = 11 m/s^2`; `Kd(w) = (A/82^2) * (1 - 0.32 w)`; speed cap 97 m/s (electric assist and rule cap; simplified).
  - Corner speed: `vc = sqrt(40 * R)` at `w = 0` (lateral grip 40 m/s^2 with wings closed). The auto-driver plans braking (45 m/s^2) to reach `vc` at the corner entry assuming wings closed.
  - **Penalty:** at brake start, time penalty `0.5 * w` s; if `w > 0.5` at brake start the round flags `run-wide` (limit violation, extra 0.5 s).
  - Ghost: same auto-driver with `w = 0` throughout.
  - Fixed step 0.01 s; results are deterministic.
- **Lap-time score:** `score = 100 * clamp(1 - (tLearner - tBest)/D, 0, 1)`; `D = max(1.0, 0.6 * (tNever - tBest))`. Round success = score >= pass score for the level.
- **Indicative golden values (prototype, +/-0.1 s; Astra regenerates):** best release about 40 m (0.4 s) before the brake point. Releasing 200 m early costs about 0.24 s on the Monza-style lap; releasing at the brake board costs about 1.4 s.

## 13. Scoring & mastery signals
- **Round score:** `100 * clamp(1 - (tLearner - tBest)/D, 0, 1)` (section 11). **Session score:** mean of rounds. **Accuracy:** successful rounds / rounds.
- **Outcome ids:** `round-1`..`round-N` (`value` = scenarioId).

| Mistake | conceptId | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `w > 0.5` at a brake start | `corner-mode` | "Wings still open when braking." |
| Released > 150 m before the brake point on a zone straight | `drag` | "Closed the wings too early." |
| Used < 60% of a zone's length | `straight-mode` | "Left part of a zone unused." |
| Pressed in a locked zone | `active-aero` | "Wings were locked in that sector." |
| Ran wide (flag) | `downforce` | "Not enough grip to turn." |

| Mastery event | conceptId | delta | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| >= 90% of zone length used across the round | `straight-mode` | +0.15 | "Used the zone." |
| All corners entered with `w <= 0.1` | `corner-mode` | +0.15 | "Wings closed for every corner." |
| Gain vs ghost >= 60% of `tNever - tBest` | `drag` | +0.10 | "Turned drag into speed." |
| No run-wide flags | `downforce` | +0.05 | "Kept grip for the corners." |
| Round success | `active-aero` | +0.10 | "Timed the wings for the lap." |
| Run-wide flag | `corner-mode` | -0.10 | "Braked with the wings open." |
| Skipped zone | `straight-mode` | -0.10 | "Did not open in a zone." |
| Hints used | round concepts | halve positives | native halves mastery gain |

- **Caps:** +/-0.30 per concept per session. Mapping to `outcomes[]`, `mistakes[]`, `masterySignals[]` is standard.

## 16. Accessibility
- **Reduced motion:** wing schematic switches with a 0.15 s cross-fade; no shake (button "Locked" label instead); freeze is a hard cut.
- **Haptics off:** visual equivalents (button pulse, gauge flash).
- **Colour-blind:** zones hatched (lines) vs locked (cross-hatch) plus text labels; rose vs gold traces differ by dash and marker shape.
- **Text scale:** to 200%; gauges reflow.
- **Tap-only:** `toggle` and `autoCloseAssist`.
- **VoiceOver:** Unity content is limited; native fallback lesson `r26-04-alt-wing-modes` (hotspot-tap "where does it open?" on a static map, binary-call "open or close here?" on frames) with equal `active-aero` mastery credit. Separate designed exercise, not a port.

## 21. Acceptance criteria (testable)
- **AC-1:** Seed 21, difficulty 3, 3 rounds emits 3 `outcomes` and one schema-valid `result`.
- **AC-2:** Determinism: same seed and scripted inputs give identical `tLearner` (1e-6 s) and signals.
- **AC-3:** For `monza-style`, an always-closed script gives `tNever` within 0.1 s of golden; a script releasing 40 m before each brake point gives `tBest` within 0.1 s.
- **AC-4:** Releasing at the brake point yields a penalty of about 0.48 s per corner and a `run-wide` flag when `w > 0.5`.
- **AC-5:** Pressing in a locked zone produces no wing movement and a mistake.
- **AC-6:** `autoCloseAssist` closes the wings 0.4 s before the brake board.
- **AC-7:** Copy: every title <= 6 words, every body <= 45 words.
- **AC-8:** All mastery `conceptId`s exist in the curriculum concept list.
- **AC-9:** Reduced motion: no eased time-scale ramp, no shake.
- **AC-10:** All targets >= 44 pt; `toggle` scheme completes a lap with taps only.
- **AC-11:** `pause`, `resume`, `abort` per bridge README.
- **AC-12:** Budgets on iPhone 13-class: p5 fps >= 50, memory < 110 MB, `launch` -> `ready` < 2 s.
- **AC-13:** Invalid config emits `error CONFIG_INVALID`.
- **AC-14:** Colour-blind mode uses distinct hatch patterns for zone and locked strips.
