## 3. Learning objective(s) & concepts taught
- **Learner-facing objective:** "You can pick a sensible lap to pit, explain why the undercut works, and know when it will not."

| conceptId | Term | After this sim the learner can... | Assessed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `undercut` | Undercut | Pit before a close rival to jump ahead on fresh tyres | Yes (primary) |
| `overcut` | Overcut | Stay out in clear air while a rival stops into traffic | Yes |
| `pit-window` | Pit window | Identify the lap range where a stop makes sense | Yes |
| `pit-loss` | Pit loss | See that a stop costs about 20 seconds and must be paid back | Yes |
| `tyre-degradation` | Tyre degradation | Read a grip bar and understand that lap time rises with age | Yes |
| `tyre-cliff` | Tyre cliff | Recognise the cliff and pit before it | Yes |
| `track-position` | Track position | Understand that being ahead is worth time on hard-to-pass tracks | Yes |
| `hard-medium-soft` | Hard, Medium, Soft | Choose a compound that lasts to the flag | Yes (d2+) |
| `two-compound-rule` | Two-compound rule | Meet the rule when planning a stop | Yes (d4) |
| `stint` | Stint | See a stint as a plan segment | Shown |

- **Out of scope:** weather, safety cars and VSC (covered by `f1.strategy.safety-car-call.v1`), fuel (no refuelling in F1), driver skill differences, undercut in the pit lane (pit stop timing is native `timing-tap`), and real tyre models. It is an arcade lap-time model.

## 4. Why Unity (tier justification)
- **Rubric answer:** A decision that depends on reading a dynamic scene: gaps, tyre grip bars and traffic change lap by lap, and whether a stop was smart only shows when the race plays forward and the cars rejoin. That is movement over time in a scene, and the camera choice (top-down race view with a live gap ruler) is part of the teaching.
- **Closest native alternatives:** `decision-scenario` with a static fact sheet (gap, tyre age, laps left) teaches the judgment for simple cases and is used in `sd-03` and `sd-06`; `estimate-slider` teaches magnitudes. This sim is used where the dynamics (the rival ageing while you rejoin) are the concept.
- **Verdict:** Justified for undercut, overcut, traffic and cliff timing; native for the rest.

## 5. Player fantasy & core loop
- **Fantasy:** You are the strategist on the pit wall, headset on, one call to make.
- **Core loop (3 rounds, about 3 minutes):**
  1. Prompt: "Your driver is 1.6 seconds behind. When do you call the stop?"
  2. Decisive interaction: watch the race run at 1 lap per second; tap **BOX** on the lap you choose (then pick a compound at d2+).
  3. Execute: the pit stop happens; the race runs to the flag (fast-forward at 4 laps per second after your stop).
  4. Freeze/explain: at the flag the gap chart replays with the moment of the undercut or the mistake highlighted.
  5. Line to say: "They undercut him, and he never got it back."
- **One mechanic:** choose the lap to box.

## 11. Scenario data set
- **Format:** JSON `pit-window-starter.json`, 12 scenarios (>= rounds x 3). Deterministic per seed.
- **Scenario schema:** `{ id, name, tags[], startLap, totalLaps, passMarginS, cars:{P:{comp,age,gapS},R:{comp,age,gapS,plan:[{lap,comp}],reacts?}, R2?:...}, trafficWindow?:{lapA,lapB,penaltyS}, legalLaps:[a,b], teachingPoint, conceptId }`. `gapS` is the time offset (negative = ahead of the reference car).
- **Race model (normative):**
  - Base lap `90.0 s`. Compound lap time: `lap = 90.0 + off + deg * age + min(3.0, k * max(0, age - cliff)^2)` with:
    - Soft: `off -0.7`, `deg 0.12`, `cliff 12`, `k 0.20`.
    - Medium: `off 0.0`, `deg 0.07`, `cliff 22`, `k 0.15`.
    - Hard: `off +0.6`, `deg 0.04`, `cliff 32`, `k 0.10`.
  - Pit lap: lap time plus `21.0 s` pit loss plus `0.8 s` in-lap; next lap (out-lap) plus `1.3 s`; tyres reset to age 0.
  - Traffic: if the pit lap is inside `trafficWindow`, the out-lap and the following lap each get `+penaltyS` (default 2.5 s).
  - Order is by cumulative time. **Hard-to-pass rule:** if a car ends a lap ahead of a car that was ahead of it at the start of the lap by less than `passMarginS` and neither pitted this lap or last lap, it is held behind (cumulative time = the other's + 0.4 s).
  - Reactive rival (d4+): the rival pits on the lap after the player pits (compound Hard) unless already stopped.
  - Two-compound rule (d4+): the two stints must use different compounds, else a `+10 s` penalty at the flag.
  - Cars advance deterministically; no randomness.
- **Scoring reference:** for each scenario the golden tool computes the final margin `M(l, c)` (seconds the player is ahead of the reference rival at the flag) for every legal pit lap `l` and compound `c`; `M*` is the best. Indicative shapes (prototype; qualitative bands are normative):

| scenarioId | Setup | Best call (indicative) | What goes wrong | Teaches | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `undercut-tight-gap` | P 1.6 s behind R; both Medium age 20 at lap 20; R pits lap 27 for Hard; 50 laps | Pit laps 21 to 24 (about +3 s ahead at the flag); pitting on lap 27 with R gives about -1.6 s; later is far worse | Copying R's lap wastes the fresh-tyre advantage; late is worse (P's tyres pass the cliff) | `undercut`, `pit-window` | undercut |
| `undercut-too-far` | Same but P 10.0 s behind | No legal pit lap gets P ahead (best about -5 s); the goal is the smallest deficit | Believing the undercut always works | `undercut`, `track-position` | undercut |
| `cliff-watch` | P alone, Medium age 21 with 30 laps to go | Pit within laps 21 to 23 (before the cliff bites) | Staying out after age 24 costs the capped 3 s per lap | `tyre-cliff`, `tyre-degradation` | cliff |
| `rejoin-in-traffic` | Like `undercut-tight-gap` with a traffic window on laps 23 to 25 (+2.5 s per lap for 2 laps) | Pit lap 21 to 22 (about +3 s), or 26 (about +1 s) | Laps 24 to 25 put you about 2 to 3 s behind after traffic | `track-position`, `pit-loss` | traffic |
| `cover-the-undercut` | P leads by 1.2 s; R pits lap 24 | Pit within laps 22 to 25 (about +1.2 to +1.4 s); staying out to lap 26 or later loses the place | Not reacting | `undercut`, `pit-window` | cover |

## 12. Freeze / explain moments
Trigger: chequered flag (after fast-forward). Camera: top-down with the gap chart; replay the moment the cars rejoin at 0.5x. Limits: Title <= 6 words; body <= 45 words.

| Case | Callouts | Title | Body | Say this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Undercut worked | Gap chart with the jump marked; fresh vs old lap-time bars | "That is an undercut." | "You pitched first and the fresh tyres gave you laps the rival could not match. When they stopped a few laps later you were already ahead. That is why teams watch the gap so closely." | "They undercut him and he never got it back." |
| Too early | Grip bar showing unused life; cliff marker far away | "One lap too eager." | "You stopped while the tyres still had plenty of life, so you paid the pit loss for a smaller gain and ended up on old tyres late. The window has a start and an end." | "They stopped too early." |
| Too late | Red cliff marker; lap time jump | "Past the cliff." | "Your tyres fell off a cliff before you stopped. Each lap cost seconds, and the rival's stop had already paid off. The window closes." | "His tyres just fell off." |
| Traffic | Cars ahead highlighted | "Right lap, wrong place." | "Pitting on that lap dropped you into a group of slower cars. You lost time stuck behind them. A lap earlier or later would have kept you in clear air." | "He came out in traffic." |
| Undercut not possible | Gap chart still showing a deficit | "Not every gap is a target." | "From that far back, fresh tyres could not make up the pit loss. It was worth waiting for the rival to make a mistake. Recognising when not to try is strategy too." | "You can't undercut from that far back." |
| Overcut worked | Gap chart; traffic window highlighted | "Stay out. Clear air." | "The rival pitted into traffic and lost time. You stayed out in clean air, kept lapping fast and jumped ahead when you stopped. That is an overcut." | "The overcut worked because the track ahead was clear." |
| Two-compound rule | Rule card; penalty text | "Two compounds, please." | "In a dry race you must use at least two different compounds. You ran the same one twice, so ten seconds were added. Pick a different tyre for the second stint." | "He has to use both compounds." |
| Summary (good) | Chips | "You found the window." | "You picked good stops and read the gap. Next time someone says 'that was a great undercut', you will know exactly what happened." | "That undercut was perfectly timed." |
| Summary (needs work) | Best round replay | "Nearly. One more call?" | "Look at the grip bar and the gap together. If the gap is small and the tyres are worn, that is your moment." | "Was that the window?" |

## 13. Scoring & mastery signals
- **Round score:** `score = 100 * clamp(1 - (M* - M_learner)/D, 0, 1)` with `D` by difficulty (section 9). Round success = score >= 70. **Session score:** mean. **Accuracy:** successful rounds / rounds. **Outcome ids:** `round-1`..`round-N` (`value` = scenarioId and pit lap).
- **Two-compound penalty and reactive-rival effects are already in `M`.**

| Mistake | conceptId | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Pit lap after the cliff lap of the player's set | `tyre-cliff` | "Stayed out past the cliff." |
| Pit lap more than 3 laps before the golden window start | `pit-window` | "Stopped before the window." |
| Pit inside a traffic window when a clear lap existed | `track-position` | "Rejoined in traffic." |
| Pit on the same lap as the rival in a tight-gap scenario | `undercut` | "Copied the rival instead of going first." |
| Chose a compound that cannot reach the flag | `hard-medium-soft` | "Compound could not last." |
| Same compound twice (d4+) | `two-compound-rule` | "Used one compound only." |
| Tried an undercut from beyond the golden reach | `undercut` | "Undercut too far back." |

| Mastery event | conceptId | delta | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round success in an undercut scenario | `undercut` | +0.20 | "Pitted first and jumped the rival." |
| Round success in overcut scenario | `overcut` | +0.20 | "Stayed out in clear air." |
| Pit lap inside the golden window | `pit-window` | +0.15 | "Chose a good lap." |
| No traffic penalty taken | `track-position` | +0.10 | "Rejoined in clear air." |
| Pit before cliff | `tyre-cliff` | +0.10 | "Avoided the tyre cliff." |
| Correct compound | `hard-medium-soft` | +0.10 | "Picked tyres that lasted." |
| Two compounds used (d4+) | `two-compound-rule` | +0.10 | "Met the two-compound rule." |
| Round failed | `pit-window` | -0.10 | "Missed the window." |
| Cliff mistake | `tyre-cliff` | -0.10 | "Went past the cliff." |
| Hints used | round concepts | halve positives | native halves mastery gain |

- **Caps:** +/-0.30 per concept per session. Standard mapping to `outcomes[]`, `mistakes[]`, `masterySignals[]`.

## 16. Accessibility
- **Reduced motion:** freeze is a hard cut; no shake; cars move with linear steps and the tower re-sorts without animation.
- **Haptics off:** visual equivalents (button pulse).
- **Colour-blind:** compounds use letter chips (S, M, H) plus colour; cars by numbers and shapes; the rival's outline is dashed; window bracket has an icon and text.
- **Text scale:** to 200%; the tower reflows to two lines.
- **Tap-only:** `lap-stepper` plus `paceAssist`.
- **VoiceOver:** Unity content is limited. Native fallback lesson `tp-07-alt-undercut` (a `decision-scenario` with graded pit laps) with equal mastery credit for `undercut` and `pit-window`. A separate designed exercise, not a port.

## 21. Acceptance criteria (testable)
- **AC-1:** Seed 11, difficulty 1, 3 rounds emits 3 `outcomes` and one schema-valid `result`.
- **AC-2:** Determinism: same seed and BOX laps produce identical final margins (1e-6) and signals.
- **AC-3:** For `undercut-tight-gap` the golden tool finds a best window that includes laps 21 to 24 (+/-1 lap), and an option of lap 27 gives a negative margin.
- **AC-4:** For `undercut-too-far` (10.0 s) no legal lap gives a positive margin.
- **AC-5:** For `rejoin-in-traffic` the best calls are laps 21 to 22 (or 26); laps 24 to 25 give a negative margin.
- **AC-6:** Hard-to-pass rule: a faster car finishing a lap within `passMarginS` ahead of a car that was ahead is held at +0.4 s.
- **AC-7:** Two-compound rule adds 10 s when violated (d4+).
- **AC-8:** Copy: every title <= 6 words; every body <= 45 words.
- **AC-9:** All mastery `conceptId`s exist in the curriculum concept list.
- **AC-10:** Reduced motion: no eased time-scale ramp, no shake.
- **AC-11:** Targets >= 44 pt; `lap-stepper` completes a round with taps only.
- **AC-12:** `pause`, `resume`, `abort` per the bridge README.
- **AC-13:** Budgets on iPhone 13-class: p5 fps >= 50, memory < 110 MB, `launch` -> `ready` < 2 s.
- **AC-14:** Invalid config emits `error CONFIG_INVALID`.
