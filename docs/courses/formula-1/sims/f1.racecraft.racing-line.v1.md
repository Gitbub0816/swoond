## 3. Learning objective(s) & concepts taught
- **Learner-facing objective:** "You can place a car on the fastest line through a corner, and say why the fastest line is the widest arc and not the shortest path."

| conceptId | Term | After this sim the learner can... | Assessed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `racing-line` | Racing line | Choose wide entry, apex clip, wide exit and explain that the goal is the largest radius | Yes (primary) |
| `apex` | Apex | Point to the apex and know a later apex helps when a straight follows | Yes |
| `traction` | Traction | Explain that a straighter exit lets the driver get on the power sooner | Yes (through exit-speed outcome) |
| `kerbs` | Kerbs | Know the inside kerb is part of the line but the white line is the limit | Yes (light) |
| `track-limits` | Track limits | Understand a line beyond the white line costs time | Yes (mistake mapping) |
| `braking-zone` | Braking zone | See where the car brakes on the speed trace | Shown, not assessed |
| `trail-braking` | Trail braking | Recognise the speed trace dipping into the corner | Shown, not assessed |

- **Out of scope:** car set-up, gears, throttle and brake pedal input, tyre wear, weather, overtaking, kerb damage, and the reason grip changes with speed (aero). The sim is a geometry-and-speed model, not a driving simulator.

## 4. Why Unity (tier justification)
- **Rubric answer:** Spatial reasoning and physics both materially help. The learner reshapes a real path through corner geometry; the consequence (minimum speed, exit speed, section time) comes from how curvature limits speed along the path and only appears when the car moves along it. The camera perspective switch (top-down to plan, chase to feel) is part of the teaching.
- **Closest native alternatives:** `hotspot-tap` (tap the apex on a diagram) teaches where the apex is but not why; `binary-call` (early or late apex?) gives the rule but no felt speed. A native fallback lesson exists for accessibility (section 16); it is a separate designed lesson, not a port.
- **Verdict:** Justified, as one of the course's five spatial anchors.

## 5. Player fantasy & core loop
- **Fantasy:** You are the driver's brain, drawing the line the car will follow.
- **Core loop (3 rounds, about 3 minutes):**
  1. Prompt: a corner appears top-down with the words "Where would you turn in, clip, and exit?".
  2. Decisive interaction: drag the handles (turn-in, apex, exit; count depends on difficulty) to shape your line, then tap **Send it**.
  3. Execute: the camera drops to chase-high; a car drives your line with a live speed ribbon.
  4. Freeze/explain: at the slowest point the scene freezes; the gold reference line appears against your rose line; the speed ribbons compare.
  5. Line to say: "He carried so much speed through that apex."
- **One mechanic:** shape a line.

## 7. Controls (touch)
| Input | Gesture / target | Hit size | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move turn-in or exit handle | Drag across the track (lateral only, snaps every 0.5 m) | visual 56 pt ring, hit >= 44 pt (padded 12 pt) | rose line redraws live; haptic tick at the white lines; white line flashes rose when the line goes out |
| Move apex handle | Drag along the inside kerb | as above | apex label shows "early / middle / late"; kerb strip lights |
| Send it | Tap the primary button (bottom, safe-area aware) | 56 pt | commits; camera cuts to chase |
| Hint | Tap "Ghost" (d1 to d3) | 44 pt | shows ghost handles; `Hint.UsedCount++` |
| Slow-mo replay | Tap "Replay" in Explain | 44 pt | broadcast-side 0.5x |

- **Accessible tap-only scheme (`controlScheme: "steppers"`):** each handle gains a selected state; two 56 pt steppers "Wider / Tighter" (0.5 m) and "Earlier / Later" (0.05) adjust the selected handle; no dragging required. Enabled automatically when `accessibility` indicates Switch Control/reduced dexterity or by configuration.
- **Orientation/safe area:** portrait. Track plan sits in the middle 60% of the height; buttons within `safeAreaInsets`.
- **Native UI not drawn by Unity:** paywall, hearts sheet, exit confirmation ("Leave game?"), permission prompts. Unity draws a small close glyph that emits `requestExit(user-quit)`.

## 9. Difficulty levels 1-5
Default: lesson `ct-04` uses d1; `ct-05` uses d2.

| Parameter | d1 | d2 | d3 | d4 | d5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Handles the learner moves | apex only | turn-in + apex | turn-in + apex + exit | turn-in + apex + exit | turn-in + apex + exit |
| Unmoved handles | auto wide (turn-in -5 m, exit -3 m) | exit auto -3 m | none | none | none |
| Scenario pool tag | `single-clear` | `single-clear`, `single-contrast` | `single-contrast` | `linked` | `linked`, `narrow` |
| Ghost hint | Always visible | On tap, max 3 | On tap, max 1 | None | None |
| Speed preview (live ribbon while dragging) | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Corners per round | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 linked | 2 linked |
| Score band `bandPct` (see section 13) | 5.0 | 4.0 | 3.0 | 2.5 | 2.0 |
| Round pass score | 70 | 70 | 70 | 70 | 70 |
| Track-limit rule | Soft clamp (line pulled inside, penalty 0.3 s) | Soft clamp, 0.5 s | Hard: penalty 1.0 s | 1.0 s | 1.5 s |
| Track width variant | wide 14 m | wide 14 m | standard 12 m | standard 12 m | narrow 10 m |
| Planning timer | none | none | none | 45 s | 30 s |

Level 1 is passable by a true beginner: the ghost shows the target, only one handle is moved, and the d1 floor applies: any line that clips the inside kerb zone (apex within 1.0 m of the inside edge) with no track-limit violation scores `max(computedScore, 70)`. From d2 upward no floor applies (a middle-apex line on `slow-90-onto-straight` costs about 3.7% and scores about 8 at d2 and 0 at d3; the same middle apex on `hairpin-late-apex` costs about 1.1% and passes with about 72 at d2).

## 11. Scenario data set
- **Format:** JSON, `racing-line-starter.json` (d1 to d3, 8 corners x 2 width variants = 16 instances) and `racing-line-linked.json` (d4 to d5, 4 linked pairs x 2 widths = 8 instances). N required >= rounds x 3 = 9; provided 16 + 8 = 24.
- **Deterministic per seed:** the seed picks a permutation without replacement; width variant is the difficulty default unless `scenarioIds` overrides.
- **Scenario schema:** `{ id, name, tags[], trackWidthM, segments:[{kind:"straight",lengthM}|{kind:"arc",radiusM,angleDeg}], entrySpeedMps, endSpeedLimitMps, station:{turnInM, exitM}, refHandles:{turnInOffsetM, apexT, exitOffsetM}, refTimeS, teachingPoint, conceptId }`. Offsets are metres toward the inside of the corner (negative = outside); `apexT` is the fraction of the corner arc length.
- **Physics model (normative):** curvature of the learner's path kappa(s) = kc/(1 - kc*d(s)) + d''(s), where kc is the centreline curvature and d(s) is the inside offset from a monotone cubic (PCHIP) through anchors [entry -80 m: outside limit; turn-in; apex clip (inside limit); exit; exit +90 m: same as exit]. Corner speed limit v(s) = min(85 m/s, sqrt(40 / |kappa(s)|)). Braking backward pass with 45 m/s^2; acceleration forward pass a(v) = 12*(1-(v/85)^2), floor 0.5 m/s^2; end-speed cap `endSpeedLimitMps`. Section time is the integral of ds/v. This is an arcade model chosen for clarity, not fidelity.
- **Reference line:** `refHandles` and `refTimeS` come from an exhaustive grid search over (turn-in offset, apex T, exit offset) with the same model, generated by an EditMode tool and stored as golden data. Values below are indicative from the author's prototype (non-normative, +/-2%); Astra regenerates the golden values.

## 13. Scoring & mastery signals
- **Round score:** `score_r = 100 * clamp(1 - deltaPct / bandPct, 0, 1)` where `deltaPct = (tLearner + violationPenaltyS - refTimeS) / refTimeS * 100`. Apply the d1 floor (section 9). Round success = `score_r >= 70`.
- **Session score:** mean of round scores, rounded. **Accuracy:** successful rounds / rounds.
- **Outcome ids:** `round-1`..`round-N` with `success`, `label` (scenario name + result), `value` (scenarioId).

| Mistake | conceptId | Description text |
|---|---|---|
| Apex T < ref apex T - 0.15 on a scenario with a long exit straight | `apex` | "Apexed too early, so the exit ran wide." |
| Apex T > ref apex T + 0.15 on `increasing-radius` | `apex` | "Apexed too late for a corner that opens up." |
| Turn-in offset > -2 m (turned in early / no wide entry) | `racing-line` | "Did not use the width on entry." |
| Exit offset inside the limit by more than 1.5 m short of the outside edge on straight-exit scenarios | `traction` | "Exit line too tight; lost exit speed." |
| Any point beyond the white line | `track-limits` | "Ran wide of the white line." |

| Mastery event | conceptId | delta | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round success with apex T within 0.1 of reference | `apex` | +0.15 | "Chose a correct apex position." |
| Round success | `racing-line` | +0.15 | "Placed a fast line through the corner." |
| Exit speed >= 97% of reference exit speed | `traction` | +0.10 | "Got a good exit." |
| No track-limit violation in the round | `kerbs` | +0.05 | "Used the kerb without crossing the white line." |
| Round failed | `racing-line` | -0.10 | "Line lost time." |
| Violation | `track-limits` | -0.10 | "Ran past the white line." |
| Hints used in round | (all round concepts) | halve the positive deltas | native halves mastery gain (Hint primitive) |

- **Per-session caps:** total absolute delta per concept <= +/-0.30.
- **Mapping:** `outcomes[]` = per round; `mistakes[]` from the table with `at` = ms since start; `masterySignals[]` from the table; `score`, `accuracy` as defined.

## 14. XP & hearts
- **`xpEarned` proposal:** +10 per successful round, +40 if all rounds are completed (native clamps to the lesson's budget). Difficulty >= 3 adds +5 for a flawless session (no violations, no hints).
- **`heartsLost`:** 1 if two or more rounds fail; max 1 per session; 0 for aborted sessions.
- **`replayAvailable`:** true after completion (Replay primitive records the deterministic frames); false when the session aborts before the first execute.

## 15. Failure states
| Situation | Learner sees | Result fields | Hearts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Failed round (score < 70) | Explain moment with the reference line and an encouraging line; "Try another corner" | outcome success false; mistakes; negative signals | Counted toward the session rule |
| Failed session (< 2 rounds pass) | Summary "Nearly there" + option to replay a scenario natively | `completed` true; score below 60 | -1 (max 1) |

| Asset missing | Native error offering retry | `error ASSET_LOAD_FAILED` recoverable | None |
| Invalid configuration | Native error | `error CONFIG_INVALID` | None |
Failure always ends in an explain moment; there are no dead ends.

## 16. Accessibility
- **Reduced motion:** cuts instead of sweeps; no camera shake; no slow-mo ramps (freeze is an instant cut); ribbons draw instantly.
- **Haptics off:** all haptic cues have a visual equivalent (line flash, marker snap).
- **Colour-blind modes:** rose line vs gold line also differ by dash pattern and end-cap shape (dot vs arrow); the kerb strip uses a hatch pattern; limit violation uses hatch plus label "Limit".
- **Text scale:** all overlay text scales to 200%; callouts reflow; minimum 13 pt.
- **Tap-only:** `controlScheme: "steppers"` (section 7).
- **VoiceOver/TalkBack:** Unity content is limited. Provide the native alternative lesson `ct-04-alt-racing-line-diagram` (hotspot-tap on a static apex diagram plus binary-call "early or late apex?") with equal mastery credit for `racing-line` and `apex`. This lesson is a separate designed exercise, not a port.

## 21. Acceptance criteria (testable)
- **AC-1:** With seed 42, difficulty 2, `scenarioCount` 3 the sim emits exactly 3 `outcomes` and one `result`.
- **AC-2:** The `result` validates against `simulation-result.schema.json`; `xpEarned` <= 40 (+5 bonus at d >= 3).
- **AC-3:** Determinism: same seed, difficulty and identical scripted inputs produce identical section times (to 1e-6 s) and identical `masterySignals`.
- **AC-4:** For each starter scenario, the reference line computed at runtime equals the golden `refTimeS` within 0.5%.
- **AC-5:** A line with no violations and apex within 1.0 m of the inside edge scores >= 70 at d1 (floor).
- **AC-6:** A line crossing the white line receives the level's penalty and a `track-limits` mistake.
- **AC-7:** Explain copy: every title <= 6 words, every body <= 45 words (test scans the definition file).
- **AC-8:** Every mastery `conceptId` exists in the curriculum concept list (`racing-line`, `apex`, `traction`, `kerbs`, `track-limits`).
- **AC-9:** Reduced motion: no camera sweep or shake; freeze is a hard cut; test asserts no `CameraRig.Shake` calls and no time-scale ramp.
- **AC-10:** Hit targets >= 44 pt in all control schemes; steppers scheme completes a full round with taps only.
- **AC-11:** `pause` freezes sim time, timers and audio; `durationMs` excludes paused time; `abort` yields a result with `aborted=true` within 1 s.
- **AC-12:** Budgets met on iPhone 13-class: 5th-percentile fps >= 50, peak memory < 110 MB, `launch` -> `ready` < 2 s.
- **AC-13:** Invalid `configuration` emits `error CONFIG_INVALID` and never starts.
- **AC-14:** Color-blind mode produces distinct dash/hatch second channels (test asserts pattern id differs).
